What stays on your device

The game saves your board, available pieces, level progress, stars, scores, personal bests and assists on your device. It also stores your language, sound and haptics preferences. These records let you pick up where you left off.

This version does not send your save to the developer or other players. There are no player accounts, developer-hosted cloud saves or cross-device sync.

Privacy choices and optional test ads in the iOS beta

On launch, build 17 contacts Google’s User Messaging Platform to check whether a privacy form applies in your region. Google’s SDK stores your choices on your device. When a form is available you can revisit it in Settings → Privacy choices. Google uses your device language and the languages available for the message.

If your privacy status allows an ad request and you choose to watch a test ad, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK requests a non-personalized test ad. Your reward is added to the game on your device. If privacy settings or the ad service are unavailable, you can keep playing and use an available free assist.

Google may process your IP address and approximate location derived from it, app or device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and performance and crash data to deliver and measure ads, detect invalid traffic and diagnose problems. The game does not ask for location permission. The earlier build 13 has no advertising SDK.

When you test with TestFlight

Apple collects beta usage and crash information and shares it with the developer to help diagnose problems. If you use TestFlight to send a comment or screenshot, Apple shares that feedback too. For invitations sent directly by email, your name and invitation email address may be visible to the developer.

Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice

Keeping or deleting a save

Your saves are kept in the app’s local storage. On iPhone or iPad, choose Delete App to remove the local save. Offload App can preserve the app’s documents and data. On Android, clear the app’s storage in device settings or uninstall it.

If device backups are enabled, a backup may also contain a copy. You can manage backup copies through your device’s backup settings. The developer cannot access the save on your device remotely.

When you contact support

When you email me, I receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. I use details such as your device model, game version or level number to investigate, reply and follow up on that request. Your email is not automatically linked to your game save.

Only include information relevant to the request. If you attach a screenshot, you can crop out personal notifications.

Who reads your support emails and when are they deleted?

As the publisher, I read and answer support requests myself. Google’s Gmail service delivers messages and attachments and stores them in my mailbox. Google may process them on servers outside Türkiye.

I process the correspondence needed to provide support and resolve the issue you report. I rely on my legitimate interest in supporting the game, taking your rights and freedoms into account. For privacy requests, I process the information needed to meet my legal obligations.

I keep the correspondence while your request is open. Within 90 days after it is resolved, I delete your messages, my replies and the attachments from my mailbox. If the law requires me to keep a record or I need it for an ongoing legal claim, I keep only the relevant records for as long as that reason applies.

Privacy requests and future changes

Email me to find out whether your data is processed, how it is used and who receives it. You can ask for errors to be corrected, for your data to be erased when the conditions for deletion are met, and for recipients to be notified of those changes. If possible, write from the address you used in your original request so I can find your correspondence.

Article 11 of Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law lists your rights. You can also contact the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board to lodge a complaint after applying to the controller. Depending on local law, you may have rights to object to processing, request a restriction or lodge a complaint with your local authority.

If the game’s data practices change, I will update this policy and its effective date.

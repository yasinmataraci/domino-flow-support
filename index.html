Player questions

How do I resume my game?

Domino Flow works offline and saves your progress on your device after each valid move. From the main menu, tap the Campaign card to return to your level, or tap Endless Mode to return to your score run. Open the Level map to revisit another level you’ve unlocked.

If something looks wrong with your progress, contact support before deleting the app. Deleting the app also removes its local saves.

Why did only part of my piece clear?

The two colors match independently. A group clears when at least four cells of the same color connect along their edges. Touching at a corner doesn’t count. The other color stays on the board until it forms a group of its own.

To rotate a piece, tap it while it’s in the tray. Then drag it into an empty space on the board.

How do I change the language, sound or haptics?

Tap the gear in the upper-right corner of the main menu. In Settings, tap Language to switch between English and Turkish. If the game opens in Turkish, look for Dil. Sound and Haptics are on the same screen.

How does using an assist affect my personal best?

In build 20, choose an available assist in the pause or results menu and finish the rewarded ad. Endless Mode offers Shuffle board and Undo. Levels offer three extra moves and Undo. Shuffle clears at least one group when enough matching tiles are present. It preserves the two offered pieces and the score. Endless Mode shows one personal best.

Can I change my gameplay analytics preference?

Yes. In build 20, open Settings → Gameplay analytics. This setting covers Campaign and Endless Mode. You can continue playing with sharing off.

Where can I change my ad privacy choices in the iOS beta?

Open Settings → Privacy choices. When Google offers a form for your device, tap the row to review your choices. The form uses your device language if that language is available for the message. If the form cannot load, you can retry later and continue playing.

What should I include in a support request?

Include your device model, operating system version, game version and what you did just before the issue appeared. Give the level number or say if you were playing Endless Mode. You can find the game version at the bottom of the Privacy policy screen in Settings. For a visual issue, a screenshot showing just the game can help.
